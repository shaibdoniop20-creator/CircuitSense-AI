1. PROJECT INCEPTION & PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================================

WHAT:
An intelligent computer-vision and material-analysis web application engineered to 
automate the sorting, inventorying, secondary valuation, and hazardous material 
assessment of discarded Printed Circuit Boards (PCBs).

WHY:
Manual dismantling and sorting of electronic waste (e-waste) is:
1. Extremely slow and labor-intensive due to miniaturized surface-mount devices.
2. Hazardous to human health due to toxic heavy metals (Lead, Cadmium, Beryllium) 
   and Brominated Flame Retardants (BFRs) embedded in fiberglass laminates.
3. Economically inefficient: High-value reusable microcontrollers and IC chips are 
   often discarded or shredded instead of being harvested for repair and maker education.

HOW:
By integrating:
- Deep learning object detection (Ultralytics YOLOv8) to localize 4 target classes:
  (1) Integrated Circuits (ICs), (2) Microcontrollers (MCUs), (3) Capacitors, 
  and (4) Connectors/Ports.
- Transparent secondary market valuation logic calculated in Indian Rupees (INR).
- A scientifically defensible dual-mode hazardous material analysis model:
  * Mode A: Empirical reference-based chemical estimation when board type is known.
  * Mode B: Insufficient data disclosure that withholds percentages when data is lacking.
- A modern "iOS 27 Liquid Glass" dark theme dashboard with interactive visual tools.

5. EMPIRICAL REFERENCE DATASETS (Pricing & Material Science)
================================================================================

WHAT:
Two structured JSON reference catalogues:
1. `data/component_prices.json` (Secondary Market Component Pricing Catalogue)
2. `data/material_references.json` (Empirical E-Waste Chemical Benchmark Dataset)

WHY:
- Transparency: An AI vision model only outputs object classes and bounding boxes; 
  it cannot invent market demand or chemical masses.
- Defensibility: By decoupling detections from verified empirical reference data, 
  all estimates are scientifically backed, transparent, and auditable.

HOW:
1. `component_prices.json`:
   - Catalogs unit price ranges in Indian Rupees (INR ₹) across specific subtypes:
     * IC: Generic SMD (₹15-₹65), Logic IC (₹20-₹85), PMIC (₹35-₹120), EEPROM (₹25-₹95)
     * MCU: Generic (₹80-₹350), 32-bit ARM Cortex (₹150-₹550), 8-bit AVR (₹40-₹180)
     * Capacitor: Electrolytic (₹3-₹15), Tantalum (₹8-₹35), Ceramic SMD (₹1-₹6)
     * Connector: Generic Header (₹5-₹25), USB/HDMI (₹15-₹60), Terminal Block (₹10-₹40)
   - Every entry records its source, condition assumptions, and reliability disclosures.

2. `material_references.json`:
   - Benchmarks 5 board classifications based on EMPA & UNEP e-waste studies:
     * Computer / Server Motherboard: 2.2% - 5.1% hazardous mass fraction (typical 3.6%)
     * Graphics Card (GPU): 1.9% - 4.5% hazardous mass fraction (typical 3.1%)
     * Controller Board (IoT): 1.4% - 3.4% hazardous mass fraction (typical 2.2%)
     * Power Supply Board (SMPS): 3.2% - 7.4% hazardous mass fraction (typical 4.9%)
     * Unknown Board: Lacks sufficient data (Triggers Mode B)
   - Quantifies valuable fractions: Gold (ppm), Silver (ppm), Palladium (ppm), Copper (%)
   - Details specific hazardous compounds: Lead (Pb), BFRs/TBBPA, Beryllium, Electrolytes.

6. COMPUTER VISION & OBJECT DETECTION SERVICE (services/detector.py)
================================================================================

WHAT:
A high-performance object detection engine implementing singleton model caching, 
YOLOv8 deep learning inference, and an intelligent OpenCV-driven Demo Mode fallback.

WHY:
- In hackathon evaluation, teams may not have completed multi-hour model training.
- The detector must never fabricate predictions or present hardcoded mocks.
- The architecture must transition seamlessly from Demo Mode to Production YOLOv8 
  the instant valid weights (`models/best.pt`) are provided.

HOW:
- Implemented `PCBDetector` using a Singleton pattern so model weights are loaded 
  only once in memory and reused across all web requests.
- Verification Logic:
  1. Checks if `models/best.pt` exists and is a valid PyTorch model.
  2. Inspects model classes: If it detects generic 80-class COCO (e.g. 'person', 'car'), 
     it automatically avoids invalid predictions and activates Demo Mode.
  3. When genuine PCB weights are present, runs `model.predict()` with configurable 
     confidence and IoU thresholds.
- Demo Mode Fallback:
  * Uses OpenCV to process the actual uploaded circuit board photograph.
  * Applies Bilateral Filtering, Canny Edge Detection, and Morphological Dilation.
  * Finds physical component contours and applies Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS).
  * Assigns target classes based on physical aspect ratios and bounding box areas.
  * Explicitly flags all returned detections with `is_simulated = True`.

7. IMAGE PROCESSING, VALIDATION & VISUAL ANNOTATION (services/image_processor.py)
================================================================================

WHAT:
A dedicated service for image sanitization, EXIF orientation correction, bounding-box 
annotation, and component-level crop extraction.

WHY:
- Security: Prevents image decompression bombs, corrupted headers, and directory traversal.
- User Experience: Produces high-resolution annotated images where labels remain readable 
  even when bounding boxes touch the image border.
- Inspection: Extracts zoomed crops of every detected component so users can inspect 
  silicon packaging, markings, and pin integrity.

HOW:
1. `validate_and_save_upload()`:
   - Validates file extension against allowed whitelist.
   - Decodes with Pillow and calls `pil_img.verify()`.
   - Re-opens and calls `ImageOps.exif_transpose()` to fix smartphone camera rotations.
   - Enforces a minimum dimension of 32x32 px and caps 4K images to prevent RAM exhaustion.
   - Saves clean, sanitized JPEG to `uploads/<analysis_id>_<filename>.jpg`.
2. `render_annotated_image()`:
   - Draws class-colored bounding boxes with tech-inspired corner brackets.
   - Calculates text dimensions and renders a solid dark pill badge behind labels.
   - Clamps coordinates to ensure labels near image edges stay fully visible.
   - Saves output to `outputs/<analysis_id>_annotated.jpg` without overwriting the original.
3. `generate_component_crops()`:
   - Crops each detected component with a 12% padding margin.
   - Saves individual images to `crops/<analysis_id>_crop_<index>_<class>.jpg`.
   - Attaches `crop_url` and dimensions to the detection dictionary.

9. SECOND-HAND MARKET VALUATION ENGINE (services/valuation.py)
================================================================================

WHAT:
Computes transparent, range-based secondary market wholesale recovery valuations in INR.

WHY:
- Optical visual detection cannot guarantee internal electrical functionality or silicon 
  authenticity without bench testing.
- Single static prices are unrealistic. A range-based model (Low, High, Midpoint) with 
  board condition multipliers reflects real-world salvage market dynamics.

HOW:
- Implemented `ValuationService.calculate_valuation()`.
- Matches detected classes against `data/component_prices.json`.
- Applies Physical Board Condition Multipliers:
  * Intact Board     : 1.00x (Standard salvage baseline)
  * Dismantled Board : 0.85x (Handling and extraction wear)
  * Damaged Board    : 0.40x (Mechanical fractures; high failure probability)
  * Corroded Board   : 0.20x (Severe chemical oxidation; requires heavy rework)
- Computes:
  * `Total Low Estimate  = sum(Quantity * Adjusted Unit Low)`
  * `Total High Estimate = sum(Quantity * Adjusted Unit High)`
  * `Total Midpoint      = (Total Low + Total High) / 2`
- Quarantines unsupported classes into `unpriced_components` with explicit warnings.

10. HAZARDOUS-MATERIAL ESTIMATION SERVICE (services/material_analysis.py)
================================================================================

WHAT:
Evaluates e-waste hazardous material content and precious metal recovery potential.

WHY:
CRITICAL SCIENTIFIC PRINCIPLE: A standard 2D optical RGB photograph cannot perform 
chemical spectrometry or measure alloy concentrations. Presenting optical detection as 
certified chemical testing is scientifically fraudulent.

HOW:
Implemented two strictly separated operational modes:
1. MODE A: Reference-Based Estimation
   - Activated when the user selects a verified board category (*Motherboard*, 
     *Graphics Card*, *Controller Board*, *Power Supply Board*).
   - Retrieves documented mass fraction percentages from empirical literature.
   - Applies the formula:
     `Hazardous Mass % = (Estimated Hazardous Substance Mass / Board Mass) * 100`
   - Details specific hazardous substances (Lead, BFRs, Beryllium, Capacitor Fluids) 
     and valuable metals (Gold ppm, Silver ppm, Copper %).
2. MODE B: Insufficient Data Handling
   - Activated when board category is *Unknown* or unclassified.
   - Outputs: *"Hazardous-material percentage cannot be reliably estimated from this image alone."*
   - Explains required laboratory testing: Handheld X-Ray Fluorescence (XRF) spectrometry, 
     board classification, and gravimetric scale weighing.
   - NEVER fabricates an arbitrary percentage.

19. SCIENTIFIC LIMITATIONS, ETHICS & DISCLOSURES
================================================================================

To maintain professional scientific integrity for hackathon judges, CircuitSense AI 
enforces three foundational principles:

1. Never Present Optical Vision as Chemical Analysis:
   RGB sensors capture reflected visible light. They cannot measure lead concentration, 
   substrate bromine content, or alloy purity. All chemical metrics are calculated 
   strictly from peer-reviewed literature benchmarks (Mode A) or transparently withheld 
   with an explanation of required XRF testing (Mode B).

2. Never Fabricate Resale Liquidity:
   Detecting an IC visually does not prove that its silicon die is operational, that it 
   is free of internal ESD damage, or that a buyer exists. Prices are presented as 
   illustrative salvage bands and conditioned on electrical bench testing.

3. Complete Transparency Regarding AI Weights:
   The application never claims custom deep learning accuracy until a model has been 
   trained on real PCB datasets. When operating without weights, it conspicuously 
   displays "Hackathon Demonstration Mode" across all views.
