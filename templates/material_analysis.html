E-Waste Material Analysis Methodology

Empirical hazardous substance mass fraction models, precious metal recovery benchmarks, and RoHS/WEEE compliance guidelines.

Essential Scientific Limitation: Standard 2D optical RGB photography cannot directly measure chemical concentrations or detect molecular bonds. CircuitSense AI enforces a strict dual-mode protocol: Mode A (Reference-Based Estimation) when board category is known, and Mode B (Insufficient Data Disclosure) when classification is indeterminate. We never fabricate chemical composition or claim optical detection equals laboratory spectrometry.
MODE A

Reference-Based Estimation

Activated when the board type is categorized (e.g. Motherboard, GPU, Controller, Power Supply). Applies empirical mass fraction data from UNEP and EMPA laboratory disassembly studies.

Formula: Hazardous Mass % = (Estimated Hazardous Mass / Total Board Mass) × 100
MODE B

Insufficient Data Protocol

Activated for unclassified, damaged, or unknown boards. The system transparently withholds percentage fabrication, declaring that RGB imagery alone cannot determine chemical composition without spectrometry.

Action Required: Handheld XRF Spectrometry & Gravimetric Scale Weighing
