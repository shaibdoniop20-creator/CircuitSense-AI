Operational Scope & Scientific Disclaimer

CircuitSense AI is developed for academic research, engineering hackathon demonstration, and operational decision support. Automated object detections indicate physical component presence and package form factor. They do not constitute guaranteed functional integrity, counterfeit verification, or certified resale value. Chemical hazardous material percentages are derived from published peer-reviewed reference fractions and empirical dismantling studies (EMPA / UNEP) and must not be used as substitutes for non-destructive X-ray Fluorescence (XRF) spectrometry or destructive chemical assays.
