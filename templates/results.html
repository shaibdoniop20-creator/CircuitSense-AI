Dismantling & Recycling Guidelines

WEEE Compliant
Pre-Harvest Pin Continuity Check: Inspect QFP/SOIC IC leads under optical microscope (10x-40x) for corrosion or mechanical bent pins before desoldering.
Thermal Budget Management: Maintain desoldering hot-air station temperature below 245°C to avoid silicon die delamination and toxic flux vaporization.
Capacitor Safe Depollution: Manually separate large radial electrolytic canisters (≥ 25mm) as a distinct chemical waste stream to avoid solvent leaching.
Precious Metal Smelter Routing: Deliver de-populated FR-4 resin substrate boards to authorized hydrometallurgical or pyrometallurgical refiners to recover gold and silver traces.
