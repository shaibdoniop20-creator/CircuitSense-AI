About CircuitSense AI

An AI-powered computer vision platform designed for internal college AI/ML hackathon evaluation and automated e-waste circularity.

Hackathon Problem Statement & Mission

Manual sorting of discarded printed circuit boards (PCBs) and electronic modules is slow, hazardous, and inefficient. E-waste workers face daily exposure to toxic lead fumes and brominated flame retardants while struggling to manually identify valuable integrated circuits.

CircuitSense AI addresses this challenge with a high-speed computer vision pipeline that automatically isolates, localizes, and inventories reusable electronic components (ICs, Microcontrollers, Capacitors, and Connectors). By combining object detection with transparent wholesale valuation and empirical hazardous material analysis, the application enables rapid circular recovery.

Project Tagline

"Detect Smarter. Recover More. Recycle Responsibly."

Technical Stack Summary

  • Vision Ultralytics YOLOv8 & PyTorch 2.x
  • Backend Flask 3.x, Python 3.11+, SQLAlchemy 2.x
  • Database SQLite Persistent Storage
  • Frontend HTML5, CSS3 (iOS 27 Liquid Glass), JS, Chart.js
  • Reports ReportLab PDF Engine & CSV Generation

Connecting Your Trained YOLOv8 Model

Current: {{ model_status.model_name }}

CircuitSense AI is architected with complete modularity. As soon as your custom PCB dataset is trained, you can activate genuine deep learning inference immediately without changing a single line of backend routing code.

# 1. Place your trained PyTorch weights in the models directory:
models/best.pt

# 2. Or configure a custom weights path in your .env file:
CIRCUITSENSE_MODEL_PATH=path/to/your/custom_pcb_model.pt

# 3. Restart the Flask app to automatically transition from Demo Mode to Production YOLOv8!

See the comprehensive ML_SETUP.md training guide included in the repository for step-by-step dataset preparation, label bounding, and Ultralytics training commands.
